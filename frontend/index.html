How it works

Write with the mouse or a pen on the left. A couple of seconds after you stop, the page is compiled and the typeset note appears on the right.

Hover a block on the right to see its region on the page, or a region to find its block.

Keys

P pen · E eraser
Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Enter compile

Corrections

Every region the compiler was sure about, most confident first. A held region is already handled; a wrong one written in at 1.00 is not.
